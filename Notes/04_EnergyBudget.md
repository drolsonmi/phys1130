## The Energy Budget
* Albedo - Rate of reflection

The Budget (If needed, __PPT__: The Energy Budget (Figure 2.17))
* Sun emits 100 units
* Atm reflects 6, clouds reflect 20, Earth reflects 4 (total of 30 units)
* 19 units absorbed in atmosphere, 71 in Earth's surface
* Atm emits 96 units that Earth absorbs
* Earth emits 117 units of energy (111 absorbed by atmosphere, 6 into space)
* Earth gains 147 and loses 117 units. __Earth has a net gain of 30 units__

If an object has a net gain of energy, the temperature will rise.

* Atmosphere emits 64 units of energy to space and 96 to earth
* Atmosphere gains 111 from Earth and 19 from space
* Atmosphere gains 130 and loses 160. __Atmosphere has a net loss of 30 units__

If an object has a net loss of energy, the temperature will drop.

Misbalance of energy. What will happen?
* Energy flows from hot to cold. How? Conduction + Convection. __= Weather__
  - Clouds and Thunderstorms
  - A single large cumulus cloud releases the same amount of energy as a small atomic bomb
  - A large thunderstorm releases the same amount of energy as several atomic bombs

__The Energy Budget__
* A net gain (surplus) of energy means temperature will rise
* A net loss (deficit) of energy means temperature will drop
* No change in energy means temperature will not change

### Horizontal surplus
The example we just did is an ideal case.
* At the equator, we absorb more energy than we lose 
  * __Net gain__ or surplus of energy
  * Hot
* At the poles, we lose more energy than we absorb
  * __Net loss__ or deficit of energy
  * Cold

What happens when there's an imbalance?
* Energy flows from hot to cold. How? Conduction + Convection __= Weather__
  - Hurricanes and Tornadoes
