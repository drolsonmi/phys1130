## Seasons
Temperature changes when the Energy Budget is not aligned.
* (Incoming Energy > Outgoing Energy) = Excess = Warm up
* (Incoming Energy < Outgoing Energy) = Deficit = Cool down

The seasons are all about this balance of energy
* Summer sees a surplus of incoming energy
* Winter sees a deficit of incoming energy

What are the factors that determine whether we have a surplus (summer) or a deficit (winter) of energy?
1. Orbit Shape
  - We have an elliptical orbit (closer to the sun in January, further from the sun in July)
2. Solar Angle
3. Length of Daylight
