## About this course
Physics
- This is a physics course - we will be learning physics principles
- Math will be used in the course, though not as much as a traditional physics course
    - Math 850, Math 1010, or an equivalent course is required
- Nearly every lecture, we will have some sort of activity where you will learn physics principles which we can apply to weather

Meteorology
- Why do we care about meteorology?
    - Fun to see / Fascinating
    - Teaches us about Physics and the world around us
    - Prepares us against damage to life and property
- Primary job: Public Safety
- Why are there mistakes?
    - Maps show weather as 2-dimensional, but is actually 3-dimensional that is always changing over time
    - Limitations in data – have to approximate

The course is separated into 4 segments. Each segment is made of multiple chapters from the textbook. From each segment, you will have the following assignmens:
- Quiz before each lecture to review the last lecture and to prepare for the next
- Homework after each chapter
- 3 exams – 1 after each segment
- Final exam covers all 4 segments
