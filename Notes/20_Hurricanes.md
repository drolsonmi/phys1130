## Tropical Cyclones
* Hurricanes = Cyclones = Typhoons = Tropical Cyclones
* Ingredients for a Tropical Cyclone
    * Warm Water
    * Coriolis Effect
* Formation & Stages
    * Thunderstorms from evaporating and rising water (*Tropical Distrubance*)
    * Frontal Wave from Africa merges with thunderstoms, causing them to rotate around a common low pressure (*Tropical Depression*)
    * Water condenses near the core, forming a large thunderstorm near the center
        * Condensation releases heat into the core, increasing temperature and pressure
        * Increased pressure cases sinking air, forming the *eye*
    * As the core strengthens and the eye forms, the winds pick up 
        * When the winds reach 35 kts (39 mph), it is classified as a *Tropical Storm*
        * When the winds reach 64 kts (74 mph), it is classified as a *Hurricane*
    * Saffir-Simpson Scale
* Naming
    
* Storm Surge
