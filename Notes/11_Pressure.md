## Measuring Pressure
- Mercury barometer
  - Difference between force of gravity and force from atmospheric pressure
  - Sucking soda from a straw
    - What if the straw is extra long?
    - Harder to suck on a straw at higher altitudes

> __Demo__: Water Barometer
> - Fill before class
> - Lower tape measure until it just touches the top of the water
> - Have 2-3 students at the top of the barometer to measure the height
> - Open the bottom and wait until the water stabilizes
> - Measure height in inH20 and convert to inHg
>   $$13.6087 inH_2O = 1 inHg \qquad 1 inH_2O = 2.49 mb$$
>   - Correction for sea level pressure: add 5.54 inHg (187.6 mb)
> - Compare to Snow Weather station

## Sea Level Pressure
Pressure at a higher altitude is smaller
* Can't compare pressure at two stations if they are at different altitudes

Pressure correction
* 1 mb per 10 m altitude
* Altitude = 1 km = 1000 m means we add +100 mb

> Go to airport and get Station Pressure, convert, and compare to Sea Level Pressure

Put these together and get a map of sea level pressures.
