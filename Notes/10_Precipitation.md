## Precipitation Processes
### Droplet Size
- Condensation Nucleus: Diameter = $0.2 \mu m$ 
- Typical Cloud Droplet: Diameter = $20 \mu m$
- Typical Raindrop: Diameter = $2000 \mu m$

Smaller droplets have higher surface tension, making it harder for them to merge.

## Liquid Precipitation
- Rain: Liquid droplets with a diameter of at least 0.5 mm
- Drizzle: Liquid droplets with a diameter of less than 0.5 mm
- Shower: Sudden downpours
  - Updraft weakens
  - If updraft reverses, you get a downdraft
- Virga: Rain evaporates before hitting the ground
