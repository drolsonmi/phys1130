## Daily Temperature Cycles
Remember
* Surplus of Energy = Temperature increase
* Deficit of Energy = Temperature decrease


> __Class Activity__: Graph the temperature and Solar Radiation data
> * Give hourly data for last 48 hours (Temperature and Solar Radiation)
> * Students graph both
> * Identify when the low temperature occurs and relate to solar radiation
> * Identify when the high temperature occurs and relate to solar radiation


### What influences temperature cycles?
Graphs from textbook show some areas have large temperature ranges and others have small ranges. What factors can influence the temperature range?
* Latitude (Graph in textbook)
* Cloud cover (Graphic in textbook)
* Humidity
