## Weather vs Climate
- What is weather? Examples of weather statements
    - Today is hot and cloudy
    - It is currently 65℉
    - It rained yesterday
- What is climate? Examples of climate statements
    - Summer is dry and hot
    - The highest recorded temperature in Ephraim is 108.0℉
    - It rained 7.0 inches last month

Segment 4 of the course will focus specifically on climate. For most of the course, however, we will be looking at what drives day-to-day weather events.

## Formation of the Atmosphere
- Origins of the atmosphere
    - Hydrogen and Helium when Earth first formed
    - Release of $N_2$, $CO_2$, and $H_2O$
    - Development of plants and production of $O_2$
        - Water Cycle
        - Carbon Dioxide/Oxygen Cycle
        - Stable $N_2$ remained in atmosphere
