Lifting a parcel through a conditionally unstable atmosphere

A surface parcel at 30 °C with a 18 °C dew point is forced upward. The environment cools at 8 °C/km, which sits between the dry and moist adiabatic rates. The parcel resists lifting until it saturates and then becomes warmer than its surroundings.
