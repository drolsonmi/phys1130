How ice crystals grow inside a cold cloud

Supercooled dropletStill liquid at −15 °C

Ice crystalFrozen at −15 °C

Unequal vapor pressure

Saturated air holds more vapor over liquid water than over ice

Vapor moves to the ice

Molecules deposit on the crystal, thinning the vapor near the droplet

So the droplet evaporates to replace it, and the imbalance remains

Until the droplet is gone

Now large and heavy, the crystal begins to fall
