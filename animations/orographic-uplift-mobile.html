Orographic Lift Lab

Humid air from the sea is pushed up the west side of a 3 km mountain and down the east side. Watch its temperature and dew point at every kilometer.

Starting conditions

Cross-section picture. A sea is on the west (left). A 3 km mountain rises from the shore, with a green, forested windward slope on the west and a dry, brown leeward slope on the east (right). An air parcel moves from the sea up and over the mountain. A cloud forms on the windward slope above the cloud base. Dotted lines mark 1 km and 2 km. The temperature and dew point appear beside each kilometer point and the cloud base as the air reaches them, and are also listed in the Readings table below.
