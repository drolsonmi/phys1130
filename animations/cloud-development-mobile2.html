When you let go, a parcel that is colder than the air around it sinks back to the ground, and a warmer one rises on its own. Real parcels overshoot and bob before settling; this is simplified. The moist rate is held at 6 °C/km and the environment cools at one steady rate. In the real atmosphere the moist rate increases with height as the air gets colder, and the environment's lapse rate changes with height.
